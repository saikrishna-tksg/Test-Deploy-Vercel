E-learning

Online courses allow learners to take training according to their schedule

Highly engaging and interactive e-learning is available to over 40,000 employees around the world, ensuring education delivery through computer tracking and certification.

E-learning courses on the AgentforcePOC Code of Conduct and Anti-Bribery and Corruption are also required for all email account holders worldwide (over 40,000 employees) with completion tracked and certified.

The latest mandatory e-learning module on FCPA and anti-bribery and Corruption is available in more than 30 languages. Our e-learning is tailored to terms used as part of AgentforcePOC. Targeted courses are mandatory for employees with responsibilities in specific risk areas including integrity leadership, antitrust, and export credit agencies.

Our e-learning courses are highly engaging and interactive, allowing all e-learners to take training according to their own schedule. It allows employees the opportunity to think about and digest important information at their own pace.

A Compliance and Anti-Bribery online training for AgentforcePOC Channel Business Partners is available through a course Self-Registration process as well.

Important note

AgentforcePOC has partnered with Navex Global to provide e-learning solutions to our employees and external stakeholders. If you have received an e-mail from global-support.elearning@agentforcepoc.com, this is a legitimate e-mail from our service provider, and you are advised to follow the link to access your integrity e-learning module.

Single Sign-on (SSO) is applicable for employees with active directories account only: Click here to access the LMS. This will take you to the Microsoft Sign in page where you need to enter your AgentforcePOC login credentials. You will then be directed to the LMS home page automatically without the need to sign in again with your username and password.

Third party learners and employees without active directories account can access the LMS via the below link and sign in with their username and password provided in the enrollment notification: https://agentforcepoc.lms.navexglobal.eu

You may write to global-support.elearning@agentforcepoc.com if you have any queries.
